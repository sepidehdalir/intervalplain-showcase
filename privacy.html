Your routine stays yours.

Privacy policy · October 15, 2026

Interval Plain does not create accounts, serve advertisements, track you, send remote analytics or upload routines. Routine names, stages, sound settings and interrupted-session positions are stored on your device. Normal device backups may include this data.

Speech and purchases

Speech uses your device’s installed voice. Interval Plain does not record your voice or request microphone access. Apple handles App Store product loading, billing, purchase verification and restoration under Apple’s policies. Interval Plain receives the verified entitlement needed to unlock Pro, and does not operate a purchase server.

Local counters

Small event counters, such as completed routine count, remain on the device. They do not contain stage names and are not sent to the developer. Apple’s own optional diagnostics and purchase records are governed by your Apple settings and policies.

Your choices

Turn stage speech or countdown off in Sound & about. Delete the app to remove its local data. Manage Apple purchases and device backups through Apple; deleting the app does not cancel or erase an Apple purchase.
