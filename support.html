For help, email hello@arvinify.com. Include your device, iOS version and what happened. Avoid sending private routine names unless needed.

Start a routine

Choose Edit routine, name your stages, enter durations in seconds and set the number of whole-routine rounds. Save, then Start routine. Use Pause, Resume or Skip; Stop asks before clearing session progress. The saved routine remains.

Fit a routine to the time available

Open Fit to available time. Enter minutes and seconds; the clear controls let you replace a value. Keep chosen stages fixed and set minimum seconds for flexible stages. Rest starts fixed. Review the original and fitted duration of each stage before Use this plan. Whole rounds stay identical; leftover seconds are shown as unused. If the budget cannot fit, increase it, reduce a minimum or unfix a stage.

The prepared plan is separate from the original. Use original timings resets it. Editing the source or selecting a different routine clears a stale plan. Share timing plan exports the calculated timing text to your chosen destination. Pauses extend the wall-clock finish.

Keep Interval Plain visible

The screen stays awake during a running routine. Locking, leaving the app, an audio interruption or disconnected headphones pauses it. Resume deliberately. Interval Plain does not promise locked-screen cues.

Sound

Open Sound & about. Enable stage names and/or the 3-2-1 countdown, check output volume and tap Test sound. Countdown applies to stages lasting at least five seconds. Long stage speech may replace or overlap a transition; old cues are stopped and stale countdowns are skipped. Bluetooth can add latency. Do not rely on audio alone where precise audible timing is critical.

Unexpected close or save error

Reopening restores a paused session at its last saved position. A short portion may repeat; cues were not delivered while closed. If saving fails, free storage and retry. If saved data cannot be decoded, the original file is preserved and edits are blocked; contact support before deleting or reinstalling.

Lifetime Pro

Pro is one non-consumable purchase for your named routine library on iPhone and iPad. Use Restore Purchases with the same Apple Account. Pending purchases unlock after Apple confirms them. Existing routines remain stored if entitlement changes. Your free routine includes time fitting, fixed stages, minimums, plan review/sharing, core timing, speech and editing.
